Localized Unified Monitoring Optimization System

Design monitoring that reduces uncertainty and supports action.

LUMOS connects probabilistic field reconstruction, uncertainty-aware monitor placement, intervention evaluation, field deployment, commissioning, and maintenance in one public environmental-planning system.

Open UnifiedTake guided tourRead quickstartAbout us

Based on professional research

One shared framework, four distinct environmental sciences

LUMOS combines continuous probabilistic fields, epistemic-uncertainty reduction, group-level information quality, domain-specific environmental behavior, and operational constraints.

  • Continuous fields rather than uniform administrative cells
  • Serious optimal-design and benchmark comparisons
  • Visible fairness and evidence-quality tradeoffs
  • Field verification, reliability, budget, and maintenance

Scientific workspaces

Choose the decision scale that matches your work

Use Unified for cross-domain planning, or open a dedicated scientific workspace for field reconstruction and site-level network design.

UnifiedAllocate budgets, compare trajectories, coordinate shared hosts, review feasibility, and manage operations across domains. HeatModel heat risk, apparent temperature, canopy and surface effects, exposure, vulnerability, and interventions. AirPlan pollutant-specific networks using wind-aware transport, source structure, reference monitors, and calibration roles. SoilCombine survey properties, depth intervals, laboratory samples, localized covariance, and field-access constraints. WaterUse indicator-specific observations, directional flow structure, upstream/downstream reasoning, and receptor roles.

Complete monitoring lifecycle

Move from uncertainty to a maintained operational network

  1. 01ReconstructEstimate the field and distinguish reducible epistemic uncertainty from irreducible variation.
  2. 02OptimizeGenerate networks under information, exposure, fairness, reliability, spacing, and budget constraints.
  3. 03EvaluatePlan treatment, control, boundary, spillover, upstream, downstream, and longitudinal monitoring.
  4. 04DeployCoordinate hosts, verify permissions and infrastructure, inspect sites, and activate reserves.
  5. 05OperateTrack installation, calibration, uptime, maintenance, failures, and replacement readiness.

Transparent by design

Every recommendation carries its evidence and its limits.

LUMOS labels observed, modeled, derived, proxied, synthetic, and fallback inputs; compares against serious baselines; reports infeasibility honestly; and preserves reproducible exports.
